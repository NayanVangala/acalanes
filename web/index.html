Runnr

When you call 911, the minutes until help arrives matter. Runnr replays real San Francisco 911 calls in a simulation. Each call goes to the unit that can get there soonest, using travel times learned from hundreds of thousands of real trips, so roads, hills and traffic are already included.

We checked it against a month of real calls it never saw. Then you can ask what if: close a station, add an ambulance, or double the calls.

Limits: it doesn't model crew staffing changes, help from neighboring cities, or hospital wait times. Use it to compare and spot patterns, not to close a real station on its own.

Data: DataSF Fire Dept Calls for Service, fire facilities, Analysis Neighborhoods, ACS 2019–2023.
