    Runnr

    When you call 911, the minutes until help arrives matter. Runnr replays a real day of San Francisco 911 calls. Each call goes to the engine or ambulance that can get there soonest. Travel times are learned from 333,000 real trips, so hills and traffic are already in them.

    Vehicles here drive in straight lines between station and call: the timing is real, the path is simplified. Change the city on the left, then run it again.

    Limits: no crew staffing changes, no help from nearby cities, no hospital wait times. Use it to compare neighborhoods and options, not to close a real station on its own.
